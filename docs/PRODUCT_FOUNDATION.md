# Nahtive 2.0 — Product Foundation

## Product statement
Nahtive is a structured Spanish-learning website that helps learners move from understanding Spanish to using it independently in everyday communication.

**Working tagline:** Learn Spanish. Actually use it.

## Audience
- Independent learners who want a clear path and consistent practice.
- Learners who want structured instruction, measurable progression, and detailed feedback.
- Learners across CEFR A1–C2 who want practical, natural Spanish.

## Differentiation
- Deliberately sequenced Spanish curriculum.
- Clear explanations and high-frequency language.
- Contextual reading and comprehension.
- Grammar taught through sentence construction.
- Translation, correction, fill-in-the-blank, and independent production.
- AI feedback that explains why an answer works or needs correction.
- Progress based on repeated evidence and retention.

## Spanish standard
- Spanish only in the first release.
- Broadly useful contemporary Latin American Spanish; label regional usage where relevant.
- CEFR A1–C2; no A0.
- Difficulty must match the content and task, not merely the label.
- Targeted support must not silently lower the lesson's intended level.

## Lesson standard
Lessons are not forced into one repeated template. Each uses an objective-led design with varied lesson archetypes, contextual vocabulary introduction and recycling, clear explanations, meaningful practice, independent transfer, explanatory feedback, and an appropriate assessment. See `docs/LESSON_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

## Feedback standard
Identify learner intent; distinguish grammar, spelling, accent, register, and regional variation; accept natural alternatives; correct the specific issue; explain it plainly; and provide one focused opportunity to apply the correction.

## Visual direction
Light-first, white and soft-neutral surfaces, blue-violet accents, readable typography, high contrast, responsive layouts, and minimal motion. Avoid childish illustration and unnecessary gamification. Dark mode is not a first-release dependency.

## Navigation
Home · My Spanish Course · Practice · Review · Progress · Settings

## Guardrails
No English/French course options in v1. No unconstrained lesson generation. No mastery claims from one quiz. No unvalidated AI lesson publication. No production data migration without tested rollback and explicit cutover approval.
